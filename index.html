Questions customers ask

Will the part fit my chair?+

Send a photo of your chair or bring the old part to the shop. We'll match it before you pay.

Can I fit it myself?+

Wheels and armrests usually just push or screw in. For gas lifts and mechanisms, ask us and we'll explain the steps.

Do you sell single pieces or only wholesale?+

Both. Buy one wheel for your home chair or a full carton for your shop.

When is the shop open?+

Saturday to Thursday, 9am to 5pm. Closed on Friday. You can send us a WhatsApp message any time.

My office chair keeps sinking. What part do I need?+

A worn-out gas lift cylinder is almost always the cause. Replacing it makes the chair hold its height again. We stock standard, heavy-duty and short gas lifts for office and gaming chairs.

Where can I buy office chair parts in Rawalpindi?+

At Ali Chair Parts, Shop # 0/789 in Kohati Bazar, Rawalpindi, near Shahid Steel Store. We stock gas lift cylinders, 5-star bases, castor wheels, armrests, tilt mechanisms and seat foam.
