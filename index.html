Help

Questions customers ask

Are your chairs imported?+

Yes. We sell imported office and gaming chairs alongside replacement parts. Ask on WhatsApp for today's models, colours and prices.

Will the part fit my chair?+

Send a photo of your chair or bring the old part to the shop. We'll match it before you pay.

Do you sell single pieces or only wholesale?+

Both. Buy one wheel for your home chair, or chairs and parts in bulk for an office or workshop.

My office chair keeps sinking. What part do I need?+

A worn-out gas lift cylinder is almost always the cause. Replacing it makes the chair hold its height again. We stock standard, heavy-duty and short gas lifts for office and gaming chairs.

When is the shop open?+

Saturday to Thursday, 9am to 5pm. Closed on Friday. You can send us a WhatsApp message any time.

Where can I buy office chairs and parts in Rawalpindi?+

At Ali Chair Parts, Shop # 0/789 in Kohati Bazar, Rawalpindi, near Shahid Steel Store. We stock imported office and gaming chairs, gas lift cylinders, 5-star bases, castor wheels, armrests, tilt mechanisms and seat foam. Coming from Islamabad? Send a photo on WhatsApp first and we'll confirm the part before you travel.
